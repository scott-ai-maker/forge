## Days 11–12: Ethical Objection Handling & Value Framing

### "How can remote coaching be as effective as having a trainer stand next to me?"
> "Traditional in-person trainers stand next to you counting reps for 50 minutes once a week, but have zero idea what you do the other 167 hours. Forge Athletic monitors your workout tempo, sleep, and recovery continuously. Through asynchronous Form Lab video audits and live WebRTC slow-motion telestration, we analyze your exact bar path and joint angles down to the millisecond. You get higher quality technical analysis without losing an hour driving to a gym."

### "I don't have a full commercial gym setup at home."
> "Forge Athletic is built specifically for real environments. Whether you have a full power rack, a pair of adjustable dumbbells in your basement, or just resistance bands in a hotel room, our periodization engine automatically adapts your exercise roster and progressive overload to the exact equipment in front of you."

### "Greater Boston personal training is usually $150/hour. Why is your monthly plan $199?"
> "Because we operate without the 50% overhead of a brick-and-mortar commercial lease, we don't have to charge inflated gym prices to cover commercial rent. You get an entire month of direct master-level coaching, 8 video technique audits, and continuous program adjustments for the price of a single in-person session."

## Days 13–14: Frictionless Onboarding & Biomechanical Setup

### Systematic Client Onboarding Flow
1. **Automated Stripe Confirmation:** Instant access to the Forge Athletic Web/PWA portal.
2. **PAR-Q+ & ERAP Capture:** Athlete inputs emergency contact and verified physical workout address (NASM VCS safety mandate).
3. **Equipment Inventory Selection:** Client selects home gym, apartment dumbbells, or commercial equipment profile.
4. **Baseline Movement Screen:** Athlete completes the 33-point computer-vision Overhead Squat recording or books their live 1:1 screen.
5. **Phase 1 Program Deployment:** Bespoke 4-week NASM OPT stabilization/strength block activated within 24 hours.
