## MASTER CORPORATE WELLNESS SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Corporate Wellness Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` ("**Effective Date**") by and between:

- **Provider:** **Forge Athletic Performance LLC**, a Massachusetts limited liability company with its principal office in Boston, Massachusetts ("**Forge Athletic**"); and
- **Client:** `[CORPORATE CLIENT LEGAL NAME]`, a `[STATE]` `[CORPORATION / LLC]` with its principal office at `[ADDRESS]` ("**Sponsor**" or "**Company**").

### 1. Scope of Voluntary Executive & Team Performance Services
1.1 **Authorized Seat Roster.** Forge Athletic shall provision up to `[NUMBER, e.g., 10]` voluntary individual athlete seats and `[NUMBER, e.g., 12]` live or virtual group biomechanics/performance workshops as specified in Order Form #1.
1.2 **Individual Athlete Onboarding Requirement.** Access to the Forge Athletic platform by any Company employee, executive, or officer ("**Participant**") is strictly conditioned upon: (a) the Participant being at least eighteen (18) years of age; and (b) the Participant independently executing Forge Athletic's direct clickwrap Terms of Service, Standalone Consumer Health Data & Biometric Privacy Policy, and ESIGN Athletic Liability Waiver & PAR-Q+ Medical Readiness Attestation.
1.3 **100% Voluntary Participation (`ADA` / `GINA` Compliance).** Company warrants that employee participation in Forge Athletic is **strictly voluntary**. Company shall not require participation as a condition of employment, penalize non-participating employees, or structure health-plan premium differentials around biometric or movement-screen outcomes without independent ERISA/HIPAA wellness-plan legal review.

### 2. Strict Employer Data Firewall (`ADA` · `GINA` · `ERISA` · `WA MHMDA`)
2.1 **Zero Individual Health Disclosure to Employer.** Notwithstanding Company's payment of subscription fees, **all individual Participant records are strictly confidential between the Participant and Forge Athletic.** Forge Athletic shall **NEVER** disclose, transmit, or make accessible to Company (including Human Resources, management, or executives) any individual Participant's:
- PAR-Q+ responses, medical history, orthopedic injuries, or medications;
- Posture photos, movement screen videos, joint-angle telemetry, or live coaching recordings;
- Body weight, body composition, nutrition logs, wearable biometrics (HRV, sleep, heart rate), or workout logs; or
- Genetic or family medical history (`Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act of 2008, 42 U.S.C. § 2000ff`).

2.2 **De-Identified Aggregate Utilization Reporting Only (`Minimum 5-Active-Seat Threshold`).** Forge Athletic will provide Company solely with de-identified, cohort-level utilization metrics (e.g., total seats activated, aggregate group workshop attendance, aggregate cohort workout completion rate) **only when five (5) or more Participants are actively enrolled in the reporting period**. If fewer than five (5) Participants are active, cohort utilization metrics are suppressed to prevent re-identification. Company covenants not to attempt to re-identify any aggregate metric.

### 3. Non-Medical Scope of Practice & Tax Characterization
3.1 **Non-Medical Athletic Performance Services.** Forge Athletic provides non-medical athletic strength, conditioning, corrective movement education, and general macro-nutrition coaching. Forge Athletic is not a licensed healthcare provider, medical clinic, physical therapy practice, or group health plan under ERISA.
3.2 **Fringe Benefit Tax Responsibility (`IRC § 61` / `§ 132`).** General athletic and executive fitness coaching memberships paid by an employer are generally treated as taxable fringe benefits unless independently qualified by Company's tax advisors. Company is solely responsible for payroll tax withholding, Form W-2 reporting, and employee fringe-benefit characterization.

### 4. Fees, Billing & Taxes
4.1 **Subscription Fees.** Company shall pay the monthly retainer (`\$3,500/month`) or upfront annual retainer (`\$35,000/year`) set forth in the Order Form via Stripe invoice or ACH within net fifteen (15) days, plus applicable state/local SaaS or digital service taxes.
4.2 **Seat Reassignment.** Company may reassign an unused or departed employee's seat license once per calendar quarter upon written notice; prior Participant records remain permanently firewalled and owned/deletable solely by the departing individual Participant.

### 5. Intellectual Property
5.1 **Sovereign Platform IP.** Forge Athletic retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Forge Athletic platform, software, AI periodization models, exercise cinematography, workshops, and trademarks. No work-made-for-hire is created under this Agreement.

### 6. Mutual Indemnification & Limitation of Liability
6.1 **Company Indemnification.** Company shall indemnify and hold harmless Forge Athletic from any third-party or employee claim arising out of Company's violation of Section 1.3 (coerced participation or unlawful wellness-plan incentives) or employment/tax law obligations.
6.2 **Cap on Liability.** EXCEPT FOR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE, NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES ACTUALLY PAID BY COMPANY TO FORGE ATHLETIC IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

### 7. Governing Law & Venue
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Commonwealth of Massachusetts**, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Exclusive venue for any commercial dispute not resolved through 30-day executive escalation shall lie in the state or federal courts sitting in **Suffolk County (Boston), Massachusetts**.
