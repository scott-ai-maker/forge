### Email 2: Overcoming Desk Fatigue & Joint Stiffness
**Subject:** The antidote to 8 hours in an executive chair

```text
Hey [First Name],

If you sit at a desk for 8 to 10 hours a day, your kinetic chain adapts to 
that seated posture:
- Hip flexors become hyperactive and shortened.
- Gluteus maximus becomes neurologically inhibited (glute amnesia).
- Thoracic spine stiffens, pulling the shoulders into protracted internal rotation.

Loading heavy weights on top of that seated posture without corrective 
stabilization is how lower back spasms and rotator cuff impingements happen.

At Forge Athletic, every program begins with our 33-point computer-vision 
Overhead Squat Assessment to identify these exact compensations before you lift.

We build an automated 4-phase corrective sequence (Inhibit, Lengthen, Activate, 
Integrate) right into your warmup so you move pain-free.

→ Run your 2-minute movement screen diagnostic:
https://forgeathletic.com/apply

Respect the kinetic chain,
Coach Gordon
```
