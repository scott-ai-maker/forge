## 3. Tier Recommendation Logic (3-Question Diagnostic)

### Ask These 3 Questions:

1. **"Where do you train and what equipment do you have?"**
   - Home gym / dumbbells / bands → Core or Plus (automated equipment filters swap movements instantly)
   - Commercial gym / comprehensive home gym → Core, Plus, or Coach Support
   - Frequent traveler → Plus or Coach Support (includes Travel Pass)

2. **"How much hands-on coach accountability do you need to stay consistent?"**
   - "I'm self-driven, just need a structured, scientific plan" → **Core ($19.99/mo)**
   - "I want exercise + nutrition dialed in, with monthly technique validation" → **Plus ($49/mo)**
   - "I want direct message access to Coach Gordon, monthly video audits, and complete oversight" → **Coach Support ($199/mo)**

3. **"Do you have acute joint issues, past injuries, or form concerns?"**
   - Minor niggles / general desk posture → Plus (includes 2 Form Lab reviews)
   - History of surgery, chronic pain, or complex barbell lifts → Coach Support or Remote Movement Screen ($159)

*Rule of thumb: Recommend ONE specific tier with clear clinical rationale.*

## 4. Operational Guardrails & Standards

- **Emergency Response Action Plan (ERAP):** Every live remote client must have verified physical address and local municipal EMS coordinates on file prior to 1:1 sessions (NASM VCS Ch 1).
- **PAR-Q+ Safety Gate:** Algorithmic intake flags cardiovascular or metabolic risks; red flags require physician clearance before heavy loading.
- **Asynchronous SLA:** Coach Support messaging is answered within 24 hours Monday–Friday. Emergency/weekend inquiries addressed first thing Monday morning.
- **Downsell Retention Protocol:** If a Coach Support client needs to reduce monthly spending, transition them seamlessly to Plus ($49/mo) or Core ($19.99/mo) to preserve their data, streak, and relationship.
