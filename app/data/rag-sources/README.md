# Forge Athletic Clean-Room Sports Science Knowledge Base

## Intellectual Property & Clean-Room Authoring Policy

To maintain strict copyright compliance (17 U.S.C. § 106) and preserve sovereign intellectual property ownership for **Forge Athletic Performance LLC**:

1. **No Third-Party Publisher PDFs in Repository:** Verbatim third-party certification textbooks, study guides, or proprietary publisher handouts must **never** be committed to `app/data/rag-sources/` (enforced via `.gitignore`).
2. **Scientific Facts vs. Protected Expression (17 U.S.C. § 102(b)):** Physiological mechanisms, biomechanical joint actions, acute training variables (sets, repetitions, tempo, rest intervals, `%1RM` intensity zones), and periodization principles are unprotectable scientific facts. All Forge Athletic RAG reference files and in-app educational explanations must be **100% original prose** authored by Forge Athletic and anchored to peer-reviewed primary literature (e.g., *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, *Sports Medicine*, *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*, *British Journal of Sports Medicine*).
3. **PAR-Q+ Attribution:** Physical Activity Readiness screening follows the validated clinical risk-stratification principles of the *PAR-Q+ Collaboration* (Warburton et al.) paired with Forge Athletic's proprietary orthopedic contraindication and Emergency Response Action Plan (ERAP) safety gates.
