## Part 1: Massachusetts Mandatory W-2 Classification Rule (`M.G.L. c. 149, § 148B`)

Massachusetts enforces the nation's strictest **ABC Independent Contractor Test**, carrying mandatory **treble (3x) damages and attorneys' fees** (`M.G.L. c. 149, § 150`) for misclassification:
- **Prong B ("Outside Usual Course of Business"):** Forge Athletic's core business **is** athletic performance, biomechanics, and virtual fitness coaching. Therefore, **any Associate Coach delivering coaching, programming, or video review to Forge Athletic clients CANNOT legally be classified as a 1099 independent contractor in Massachusetts** (or California `AB 5` / New Jersey).

### Mandatory Hiring Protocol for Associate Coaches
1. **Onboard 100% of Associate Coaches as W-2 Employees** (part-time hourly, per-session W-2 Piece/Hourly rate meeting minimum wage + overtime rules, or full-time exempt salaried) via Gusto / Rippling.
2. Maintain **Massachusetts Workers' Compensation Insurance** (`M.G.L. c. 152`), Paid Family and Medical Leave (`MA PFML`), and Unemployment Insurance (`DUA`) prior to the first day of work.
3. Verify active **NASM-CPT / CSCS** and **Adult CPR/AED** certifications annually and endorse each W-2 coach onto Forge Athletic's **Professional Liability (Fitness/Wellness E&O)** policy.
4. Reserve **1099 Independent Contractor Agreements** strictly for non-coaching vendors outside Forge Athletic's usual course of business (e.g., outside CPAs, attorneys, photographers, or external software auditors), always paired with an explicit **Work-Made-For-Hire & Irrevocable IP Assignment** clause (`17 U.S.C. § 101`).
