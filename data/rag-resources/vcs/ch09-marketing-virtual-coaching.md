### Online Marketing Best Practices and Professionalism

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 2Online Marketing Vehicles and StrategiesOnline Marketing Best Practices and ProfessionalismTo truly be a successful online marketer, a fitness professional must know more than the various digital marketing platforms. They must also have a strong knowledge of which methods are most effective. The following section will discuss and review those methods and provide specific examples of implementation strategies.
Search Engine OptimizationOn any given day, there are over 2 million searches conducted on Google alone (Patel, 2019). This is why search engine optimization, or SEO, has become an important factor in marketing and searchability. SEO is a method of improving a website's ranking during organic, unpaid searches. Research shows the first five results on Google get 67% of the clicks (Patel, 2019). In such a noisy, online world, ranking high on the list of these searches is paramount. One of the best and most effective ways to increase SEO is by producing quality content. Items such as blog posts and e-books can organically drive people to the site, which will, in turn, increase overall ranking in the eyes of the search engine.
Engagement Methods for Digital MarketingTo produce engaging content, a fitness professional should view their actions strategically, rather than use a one-size-fits-all approach. When thinking about posting a piece of content, a marketer needs a system to determine the effectiveness of their post. The fitness professional must set clear metrics of success for their digital marketing efforts and campaigns. These metrics should align with their business goals. Fitness professionals can use analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Sprout Social to monitor what is and what is not working and know how to adjust accordingly.

### Webinars

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 2Online Marketing Vehicles and StrategiesWebinarsWebinars are one of the fastest-growing digital marketing methods. Webinars are live or prerecorded virtual conferences or presentations. Webinars can help establish fitness professionals as an authority in a specific topic or niche. For example, if a nutrition coach is well-versed in weight loss, he or she could host a webinar on the topic of intermittent fasting.
Webinar StructureThere are typically two types of webinars that can be run—paid and free. Although a paid webinar can make money, it can also potentially limit the number of people who are able to attend. On the other hand, hosting a webinar may aid in lead qualification as only the most serious potential clients will be interested in attending. One possible strategy when hosting webinars is to host it for a small fee and then reveal a back-end offer near the end of the webinar. That offer could be a bit more exclusive for clients who are interested in learning more about the topic or possibly purchasing a higher-end service. Using that same example of the nutrition coach, a potential strategy could be to offer a nutrition consultation or a meal plan to anyone who attended the webinar. Web-based services like Zoom and Webinar Ninja allow any fitness professional to easily host a webinar.

### YouTube

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 2Online Marketing Vehicles and StrategiesYouTubeWith over 2 billion users worldwide, YouTube is one of the most popular forms of media for consumer video consumption. Many online fitness professionals have gained popularity by dedicating their time to developing a YouTube platform. Creating a YouTube channel is a great way for a fitness professional to build authority in their respective field. ART PAL/Shutterstock.comBlogsBlogs are one of the best ways to create authoritative content and drive traffic to a website. Additionally, traffic created from blogs helps improve website visibility, search engine ranking, and ultimately SEO.
Methods for Increasing Blog Visibility In the early inception of blogging, visibility was a relatively easy thing to obtain. However, as more people have started blogging, visibility has become much harder to achieve. By some estimates, there are over 600 million blogs on the Internet today (Petrov, 2020). With the large number of blogs available, the question then becomes, “How does the fitness professional’s blog stand out?”
Vasyl Nagernyak/Shutterstock.comHere are a few methods to gain greater visibility through blogging:


    Consistency – To gain a loyal audience, a blogger must post consistently. Try setting a blog-release schedule.
    Timing – Publish blog posts on high-traffic social media days. For example, releasing a blog on a Tuesday, normally a high-traffic day, will likely yield greater engagement than a blog posted on a Saturday.
    Naming a Post – All digital marketing vies for consumer attention. The title of a blog post is the first thing someone sees and will often determine whether they choose to read on. Titles that begin with the phrases “How to…” and “Here are 5 ways to” tend to get more traffic over other more general titles.
    Collaborations – Collaborating with other fitness professionals is a great way to increase the reach of a blog post. Interviews and guest posts are all methods that increase the shareability of a blog post. The post then gains exposure for not only the fitness professional’s followers but also to the followers of the other collaborator.

### Proper Messaging

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 2Online Marketing Vehicles and StrategiesProper MessagingMessaging varies depending on the social media platform and on the message being delivered. Generally, it is important to keep messaging concise and direct. Whenever posting digital content, proper messaging and professionalism is crucial. This is true for both professional and personal social media accounts as a fitness professional is ultimately a direct representation of their business. Proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation should be used in all posts. Additionally, most posts should be done in an authoritative tone to convey the expertise of the fitness professional.
Proper Use of Visuals As the use of social media and digital marketing has grown, it is more important now than ever for the fitness professional to incorporate quality visuals into their social media marketing. The standard has been raised as more people become adept at visuals and social media. Camera phones have made this easier. With most phones now equipped with high-quality cameras, it is often unnecessary to hire a photographer to take professional photos. In the past, the creation of graphics required expensive software with a steep learning curve. However, now there are many graphic software products available, such as Canva or PicMonkey that make it easier and more cost-effective (and in many cases free) to design and edit high-quality marketing material.
Tero Vesalainen/Shutterstock.comIn addition to the quality of the visuals, the formatting of video and pictures is also important. For example, consider recording an exercise video with the intent of posting it on multiple video media platforms. Shooting the video in portrait mode (vertical orientation) is great for posting on an Instagram feed but it is not the ideal camera orientation for posting videos on YouTube. Landscape mode (horizontal orientation) is the preferred method for posting videos on YouTube and Facebook (Figure: Video Format). Videos shot in portrait mode leave extra space in the margins of the video when viewed on a widescreen, which can be distracting to the viewer.

### Word-of-Mouth Marketing

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 2Online Marketing Vehicles and StrategiesWord-of-Mouth Marketing Despite the advancements in technology and digital platforms, word-of-mouth marketing continues to be an incredibly effective form of marketing. According to research, 67% of consumers are based on word of mouth (Karlíček et al., 2010). As a fitness professional, developing trust and a strong referral network from current clients is a good way to grow a sustainable online business.
Referrals Offering existing members some type of incentive may encourage referrals. Ideally, clients will refer potential customers regardless of an incentive, but offering one can certainly help. One way to incentivize referrals is to offer a gift card or credit to clients who refer new clients to a business. A potential benefit of offering gift cards, as opposed to discounting an existing client's service, is that money spent on gift cards could potentially be written off for tax purposes, depending on the business situation. Additionally, gift cards do not devalue the price of training options.

### Testimonials

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 2Online Marketing Vehicles and StrategiesTestimonialsTestimonials tap into a powerful, scientific marketing principle—the principle of social proof. The principle of social proof suggests that individuals observe and assume the behaviors and actions of others to determine what is correct or to find out what other people think is correct (Cialdini, 2006). If a potential customer sees that someone like them has had good results and a positive experience, they are more likely to choose that same service over another one. 
Obtaining TestimonialsBoth written and video testimonials are excellent pieces of marketing material. There is some debate over which method is more effective. Luckily, with transcription services readily available, obtaining both is easy and relatively inexpensive. One way to obtain both types is to record a video testimonial of a client’s experience and then send the audio file to a transcription service to have it converted into written format.
When gathering testimonials from clients, fitness professionals should provide a guide to follow so results come out clean and succinct. This guide may include recommendations for talking points and sample questions. The following are examples of questions that may yield a strong testimonial:


    What was your experience when you first started working with (insert name of fitness professional and business)?
    What was the biggest challenge you faced?
    What is your experience like now?
    What is your favorite thing about training with (insert name of business)?


Questions like these create a story or a hero’s arch for the client testimonial. These questions relate their struggles and connect their story to others and potential clients who will likely share some of the same challenges.
Helpful HintNot sure whether to have a client do a written or a video testimonial? Why not do both?

With online transcription services, fitness professionals can record the video and then send it to a transcription company to have it converted into text. Typically, these companies charge a per-minute fee, and, for a short video, it is a valuable time saver. Once the fitness professional has the transcription and video, they can easily post and share them on various platforms.
MerchandiseMerchandise is often an underrated marketing tool. Merchandise is anything that clients can use or wear that represents a brand. Merchandise transforms the client into a walking billboard. Examples of effective merchandise for fitness businesses include t-shirts, stickers, and protein shaker bottles. The key to any sort of merchandise is to make sure the brand is clear and visible. Merchandise can also be an effective way to increase client retention. For example, if a client reaches a certain weight-loss goal, a great strategy would be to send them a t-shirt for their success and have them post a picture of themselves wearing it. This not only increases client retention but also helps build social proof for the brand.

### Limitations of ROI

Chapter 9Marketing Virtual Coaching Lesson 3Evaluating Marketing EffortsLimitations of ROI Not all marketing efforts are afforded an immediate return on investment. A fitness professional may host a webinar in June and then be contacted by a potential client in November for online training. It can often be difficult to pinpoint if that person came in because of a particular marketing effort. Always expecting immediate ROI from a marketing effort can also be problematic. Some marketing efforts need time to take effect. In general, while it is important to understand a campaign’s ROI, it may be more ideal to have a long-term mindset when it comes to determining the true value and success of marketing efforts.
