Reserve **1099 Independent Contractor Agreements** strictly for non-coaching vendors outside Forge Athletic's usual course of business (e.g., outside CPAs, attorneys, photographers, or external software auditors), always paired with an explicit **Work-Made-For-Hire & Irrevocable IP Assignment** clause (`17 U.S.C. § 101`).
