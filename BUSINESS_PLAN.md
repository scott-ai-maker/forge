### Chapter 1: Scope of Practice, Remote Safety & ERAP
- Comprehensive Emergency Response Action Plan (ERAP): Prior to commencing live training, every client provides a verified physical training address and local EMS dispatch coordinates.
- PAR-Q+ Algorithmic Triage: Automated health status screening halts program enrollment whenever cardiovascular, orthopedic, or metabolic red flags emerge, requiring physician clearance before activation.
- Data Privacy & Security: High-level encryption (AES-256) of biometric records, SOAP advisory logs, and movement video captures.

### Chapter 2: Scalable Business Architecture (In vs. On the Business)
- Transitioning from an hourly trainer (working *in* the business) to an enterprise fitness entrepreneur (working *on* the business) by automating repeatable systems.
- Core Service + Modular Variations + High-Value Add-Ons:
  * Standalone Exercise Periodization
  * Standalone Nutrition & Metabolic Blueprint
  * Complete Hybrid Coaching (Exercise + Nutrition + 1:1 Live Studio)
- 3-Month Minimum Subscription Commitment: Mitigates churn, protects Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), and provides sufficient biological runway for meaningful neuromuscular adaptation and body recomposition.
- Downsell Retention Architecture: If an athlete faces temporary financial constraints, the system automatically routes them to a lower-tier maintenance plan (e.g., Core at $19.99/mo) rather than an outright cancellation, preserving the relationship and CLV.

### Chapter 3: Technology Infrastructure & Digital Virtual Studio Ergonomics
- The 100% Digital Virtual Studio: Replacing brick-and-mortar physical rooms with an encrypted, broadcast-grade WebRTC 60fps Consultation Studio.
- WebRTC Peer-to-Peer 60fps Consultation Studio: Sub-100ms latency audio/video streaming via Supabase Realtime signaling.
- Optical Unmirrored Geometry: Virtual background canvases maintain true optical orientation so brand typography reads correctly from left to right, while mirroring only the coach’s foreground to preserve natural proprioceptive intuition.
- Screen Wake Lock & Offline Resilience: Mobile clients cache workout sets in IndexedDB locally, surviving basement and signal-deprived gym environments.

### Chapter 4: Virtual Communication & Coaching Strategies
- Empathetic Motivational Interviewing: Meeting clients where they are, resolving ambivalence, and fostering internal locus of control.
- Structured Response Windows: 24-hour asynchronous messaging SLA for Coach Support tiers; 4-hour SLA for VIP private retainers.

### Chapter 5: Virtual Movement & Postural Assessments
- 33-Point MediaPipe Computer Vision Screening: Remote Overhead Squat Assessment (OHSA) evaluates dynamic knee valgus, excessive forward lean, lumbar extension, asymmetrical weight shifts, and foot pronation.
- Automated Corrective Continuum: Converts detected postural distortions into immediate 4-phase corrective prescriptions (Inhibit, Lengthen, Activate, Integrate).

### Chapter 6: Session Implementation & Real-Time Biomechanical Cuing
- Multi-Sensory Coaching: Auditory cadence beeps, visual tempo pulses (e.g., 4-2-1-1 tempo for stabilization), and real-time slow-motion telestration.
- Bar Velocity & Power Profiling: Accelerometer and camera tracking to quantify concentric bar speed and detect central nervous system fatigue.

### Chapter 7: Wearables & Telemetry Integration
- Continuous Biometric Ingestion: Apple HealthKit, Health Connect, Garmin, and Polar telemetry.
- Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR): Compares 7-day acute volume tonnage against 28-day chronic baseline to autoregulate volume and prevent overtraining (Sweet Spot: 0.80–1.30; Deload trigger: ≥1.50).

### Chapter 8: Inbound Problem-Solving Sales
- Sales as Ethical Problem Solving: Identifying customer pain points (lack of time, chronic aches, inconsistent routines) and presenting clear, structured solutions.
- Prospect -> Lead -> Customer Pipeline: Automated lead capture, interactive diagnostic quiz, and frictionless checkout.
- 3-Tier Value Ladder (Good, Better, Best): Removing decision friction and cognitive overload.

### Chapter 9: The 4 Quadrants of Digital Marketing
